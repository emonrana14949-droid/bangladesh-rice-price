LIMITATIONS
  - The monthly series is a mean across all markets that reported in that month; the number of reporting markets changes over time, so early years rest on fewer markets.
  - Only coarse rice, retail, BDT/kg is analysed. Fine/medium rice, wholesale prices and paddy prices are in the raw file but not used here.
  - Prices are nominal BDT — they are NOT adjusted for inflation, so part of the long rise is general price inflation.
  - The forecast test is only 12 months; a longer test would be needed before trusting any model.
  - WFP/HDX data can be revised after publication; re-download the file to get the newest version.
